# What's new in Voie Libre

What changed in each version, written for the people who use the app. The newest comes first. The code, the reasons behind each change and the technical notes are in [docs/features](docs/features/README.md).

## 0.4.2 — November 14, 2026

- **Import more kinds of files, several at once.** "Import…" now takes GPX files, FIT files (from a watch or a bike computer) and the routes file made by "Export all…", and you can pick many files in one go. A short message sums up what was added and names the files that could not be read.
- **Pop-ups for file actions.** Importing, exporting all your routes, choosing a file format for one route and deleting a route now open a pop-up, instead of changing the list in place. Renaming still happens in the route's card.
- **See at a glance which routes are shared.** A route with an active link shows a badge "Link active until…", and its button reads "Manage sharing". "Delete the sharing" stops the link right away.

## 0.4.1 — November 14, 2026

- **One kind of link, easier to understand.** Sharing now always makes a short link whose copy is kept for a day, a week or a month, then removed. The older link that carried the whole route inside its address is gone.
- **"Share…" saves the route first** if you had not saved it yet, so you never have to think about it.
- You can share the same route again as soon as its link has expired.

## 0.4.0 — November 14, 2026

- **Anonymous visit counts.** The app now counts visits and the use of a few features, in aggregate: no cookie, no identifier, nothing about you, your places or your routes. You can switch it off in the "About" window, and it is off when your browser asks not to be tracked.
- **Short links work for long routes.** A long route used to be refused when you made a link.

## 0.3.2 — November 14, 2026

- **Share a route.** Copy a link, show a QR code to scan with a phone, or use your phone's share menu. Whoever opens the link is asked what to do: save the route, just look at it, or export it as a file.
- **Back up all your routes in one file**, and bring them back on another device.
- **The map opens on all of France** the first time, then where you left it.
- **Install the app** on your phone or computer, from the "About" window, to open it from its own icon.
- On a phone, the panel takes the whole width, you can swipe it from anywhere, and swiping down no longer reloads the page. The full screen button, which did not work on Android, is gone.
- Better previews when the site is shared in a message, and easier to find in search engines.

## 0.2.2 — November 13, 2026

- **Build a route point by point.** Each click adds a place after the last one, until you press "Finish here". "Continue the route" opens it again.
- **Arrows on the route** show which way it goes: an arrow where you ride a stretch once, a double arrow where you ride it both ways.
- **Colour the route** by the kind of way (paved road, cycle track, unpaved way, path) or by how steep it is.
- **A closer look at the elevation profile.** Select a stretch to read its distance, climb, descent and grade in a bubble, with a button to zoom the map on it. Descents can be coloured or left plain, as you prefer for each sport.
- **Loops by time.** Ask for a loop of a given duration, based on your own speed for each sport, and choose how many alternatives to compare.
- **A phone-friendly panel.** On a phone the panel is a sheet you pull up and down, with a short summary when it is folded, and the header makes room for the map.
- A clearer route panel, a side panel you can widen, and a "My position" button at the end of each place field.
- A welcome screen on your first visit, a way to support the project, and the version number in the "About" window.

## 0.1.0 — November 12, 2026

The first public version: plan road, gravel and trail routes on a map of France, with loops, elevation profile, shaded relief and optional aerial photos. Routes are kept on your device and can be exported as GPX or FIT files. It works offline for the areas you keep, in English and in French, with no account and no advertising.

_Versions 0.2.0, 0.2.1, 0.3.0 and 0.3.1 were never put online; what they held is in the next version listed here._
