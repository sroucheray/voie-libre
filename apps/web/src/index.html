Voie Libre

A free, open source route planner for road cycling, gravel riding and trail running. Pick a start and an end on the map, choose your sport, and get a route along the roads and paths that suit it, with its elevation profile and the surface of the way.

  • Loops by distance or by duration, with a climb to aim for.
  • Several alternatives, stops you can move and reorder.
  • GPX and FIT export for your phone, bike computer or watch.
  • Offline areas, and an app you can install. No account.

Voie Libre est un planificateur d'itinéraires gratuit et libre pour le vélo de route, le gravel et la course en sentier : boucles par distance ou par durée, profil d'altitude, revêtement, export GPX et FIT, cartes hors ligne. Sans compte.
